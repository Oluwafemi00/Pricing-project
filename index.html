Frequently asked

How are exchange rates calculated? +
We fetch live exchange rates from the Frankfurter API, which sources data from the European Central Bank. Rates are refreshed every time you load the page. If the API is unavailable, we fall back to recent cached rates.
Can I switch plans at any time? +
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. We'll prorate any difference automatically.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. Invoicing is available for Expert tier customers.
Is there a free trial? +
Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Cancel any time during the trial and you won't be charged.
